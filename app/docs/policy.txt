
CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

1. Refund Policy
Customers can request a refund for eligible orders within 7 days
of delivery. The order must meet the company's refund conditions.
Refund eligibility must be verified before approval.

2. Delivery Policy
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.
If an order has not arrived after the expected delivery window,
the support team should check the tracking information.

3. Payment Policy
If a customer is charged but the order is not confirmed,
the support team must verify the payment transaction and order status.
A refund must not be promised until the transaction is investigated.

4. Account Policy
Customers who cannot access their account should use the
password-reset process. If that fails, the support team should
verify the customer's identity before further assistance.

5. Technical Support Policy
Customers experiencing application errors should provide the
error message, device information, and steps to reproduce the issue.
The support team should investigate reproducible technical problems.
